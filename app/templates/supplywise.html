The problem this solves

Nearly 80% of procurement executives say the top blocker to catching supplier risk in time is lack of visibility — the data exists, but it's scattered across spreadsheets and systems and isn't fast to query or explain (2025 supply chain risk surveys, Xeneta / Sphera). SupplyWise closes that gap: ask a question in plain English, get an explainable risk answer, and get the supplier's real phone number in the same step — instead of three separate lookups.
