# Category Risk Notes: Metal Fabrication and Chemicals

Metal Fabrication suppliers have historically shown the widest variance in delivery
performance across all SupplyWise supplier categories, largely due to dependency on raw steel
and aluminum spot pricing and single-source CNC machining or bearings/tooling capacity.

Chemicals/coatings suppliers are subject to additional regulatory inspection at dispatch,
which can add 3-5 days to lead time during peak compliance audit periods (typically Q1 and Q3).
Procurement officers should build this buffer into planning for any Chemicals order exceeding
1000 units.

Both categories are flagged in this document as requiring closer risk monitoring than the
company-wide average, and any supplier in these categories with a defect rate above 4% should
be escalated to the supply-chain analyst for review, even if it has not yet crossed the formal
High Risk threshold.
