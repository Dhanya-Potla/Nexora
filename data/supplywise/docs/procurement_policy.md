# SupplyWise Procurement Policy

## Supplier Qualification
All suppliers must maintain an on-time delivery rate above 80% and a defect rate below 5%
to remain in the "Preferred Supplier" tier. Suppliers falling below these thresholds for two
consecutive quarters are moved to a Corrective Action Plan (CAP) and reviewed monthly.

## Risk Categories
- **Low Risk**: on-time delivery rate above 90%, defect rate below 2%.
- **Medium Risk**: on-time delivery rate between 75% and 90%, or defect rate between 2% and 6%.
- **High Risk**: on-time delivery rate below 75%, or defect rate above 6%. High-risk suppliers
  require dual-sourcing backup for any order above INR 100,000.

## Purchase Order Approval
Purchase orders under INR 50,000 can be approved by a procurement officer directly. Orders
between INR 50,000 and INR 500,000 require supply-chain analyst sign-off. Orders above
INR 500,000 require operations-team approval and a documented risk assessment of the supplier.

## Lead Time Expectations
Standard lead time across categories should not exceed 14 days. Suppliers in Metal Fabrication
and Chemicals categories are expected to declare lead time at the time of quotation, since these
categories historically show the highest variance in delivery performance.
