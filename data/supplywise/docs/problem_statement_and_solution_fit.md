# The Problem SupplyWise Targets: Supplier Visibility

## The Evidence
Industry procurement surveys consistently name lack of supplier visibility as the top operational
problem in supply chain management, ahead of cost and even ahead of raw disruption frequency:

- Nearly 80% of supply chain executives say they need to invest in better visibility tooling,
  because a lack of insight into supplier processes and status leads to unanticipated
  disruptions (2025 global supply chain risk research, reported by Xeneta and Procurement Magazine).
- 68% of procurement organizations cite supply chain disruption itself as their top challenge,
  but the underlying reason disruptions catch teams off guard is that the information needed to
  act - which supplier is at risk, why, and how to reach a backup - is scattered across systems
  and people instead of queryable in one place.
- 21% of procurement decision-makers and 18% of senior leaders admit they simply lack the systems
  to monitor and manage supplier risk at all (Sphera AI-Powered Supplier Risk Management Survey,
  2025).

The common thread: the data usually already exists somewhere (spreadsheets, ERP exports, supplier
onboarding PDFs, email threads) but it is not fast to query, not explainable when it does surface
a risk, and contact information is often the last thing found when a supplier needs to be replaced
under time pressure.

## How SupplyWise Fixes This
SupplyWise is built specifically against that visibility gap, not just as a general chatbot:

1. **Natural-language retrieval over procurement documents and structured supplier data (RAG)** -
   a procurement officer can ask "which suppliers are high risk?" or "who can I call about a
   coating supplier?" and get an answer grounded in real records in seconds, instead of manually
   cross-referencing spreadsheets.
2. **Explainable risk, not a black-box score** - every risk level shows the exact thresholds it
   crossed, so a decision-maker can act immediately rather than escalate for clarification.
3. **Contact information surfaced by default** - every supplier record carries a real phone number
   and address, so "find a backup supplier" and "call that supplier" are the same step, not two
   separate systems.
4. **Semantic supplier/product matching** - a buyer can describe a need in plain language and find
   a matching supplier even when the supplier's own listed category or product name uses different
   words, closing the gap between what buyers ask for and how vendor data happens to be labeled.

This is the specific, evidence-backed problem SupplyWise's RAG pipeline is designed to solve:
turning fragmented, hard-to-query supplier information into fast, explainable, contactable answers.
