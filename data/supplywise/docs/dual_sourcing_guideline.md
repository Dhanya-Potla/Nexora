# Dual-Sourcing Guideline

For any product category where a single supplier accounts for more than 40% of total order
volume, procurement must identify and qualify at least one backup supplier within the same
category. This is mandatory for High Risk suppliers regardless of order volume share.

Dual-sourcing reduces exposure to a single point of failure and gives the supply-chain analyst
leverage during price renegotiation. Backup suppliers do not need to match the primary
supplier's lead time exactly, but must be within the Preferred or Approved tier.
